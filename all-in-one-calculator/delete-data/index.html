Delete your data

VectraCalc — All-in-One Calculator, by VectraCalc  ·  Updated 2026
In short

VectraCalc has no account and no server. Everything the app stores lives on your device, and you can erase all of it directly in the app or by uninstalling. Advertising data collected by Google can be reset through your device's Google settings. Steps for both are below.

1Delete your in-app data (history, saved, favorites, settings)

This data is stored only on your device. To delete it in VectraCalc — All-in-One Calculator:

  1. Open the app and go to Settings (bottom navigation).
  2. Scroll to the Data section.
  3. Tap Clear history, Clear saved calculations, and/or Clear favorites. Each asks you to confirm.
  4. To restore all preferences to defaults, tap Reset settings.

Or delete everything at once by uninstalling the app — this removes all VectraCalc data from your device immediately.

2Delete advertising data collected by Google

To keep the app free, VectraCalc shows ads via Google AdMob, which collects your device's advertising ID and an approximate (IP-based) location. This data is held by Google, not by us. You can control and delete it:

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID: on your device, open Settings → Google → Ads, then choose Delete advertising ID or Reset advertising ID.
  • Remove ads entirely: upgrade to VectraCalc Pro in the app — Pro users are shown no ads.
  • Manage data held by Google: use Google's account privacy tools at myactivity.google.com and see the Google Privacy Policy.
