Who we are

Software the way it used to feel: yours.

VectraCalc started with a simple frustration: too many everyday apps demand an account, a connection, and your data — just to do basic things. We think a tool should work the moment you open it, whether you're on a plane, in a basement classroom, or on a job site with no signal.

So we build offline-first. Everything our apps do happens on your device. Nothing you type is sent anywhere, and nothing needs a sign-in. When we do use online services — like ads that keep our apps free — we're upfront about exactly what they collect.

Our first app, VectraCalc: All-in-One Calculator, brings a whole toolkit into one polished, offline experience. It's the template for everything we make next: deep on capability, light on demands.
