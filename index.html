Good to know

Questions, answered.

Does it really work offline?
Yes — completely. Every calculator, graph, and step-by-step runs on your device. There's no sign-in and nothing you enter is ever sent anywhere.
What's free, and what's Pro?
All 209 calculators are free to use. Pro removes ads and unlocks unlimited step-by-step solutions, interactive graphs and 3D, exporting solutions, and unlimited saved calculations and favorites.
If I buy Pro and reinstall, do I keep it?
Yes. Pro is tied to your Google account through Google Play. Reinstall the app, or open it on another device signed into the same account, and Pro restores automatically — no extra login.
How do I cancel a subscription?
Subscriptions are managed in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions. You can cancel anytime and keep Pro until the end of your billing period. The Lifetime plan is a one-time purchase with nothing to cancel.
Which devices are supported?
VectraCalc is an Android app, available on Google Play. It adapts to phones and tablets, and defaults to a comfortable dark theme.
